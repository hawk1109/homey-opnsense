See how your OPNsense firewall is doing, right in Homey.

This app connects to the OPNsense API on your local network and turns the firewall into a Homey device, with a dashboard widget that shows everything at a glance.

What you get
• Internet status, latency and packet loss from the gateway monitor, plus live download and upload speed
• CPU load, memory, temperature, disk and firewall states, each with its own warning threshold
• Every service on the firewall: get notified when one stops, and restart it from a Flow
• Flows when the internet goes down or comes back, when the firewall becomes unreachable or reboots, and when any problem starts or is resolved
• A dashboard widget with 24-hour latency and throughput charts, resource tiles, services and recent events

Your API key never leaves your network, and the app only talks to a firewall presenting the certificate you approved.
